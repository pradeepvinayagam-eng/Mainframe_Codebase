       IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
       PROGRAM-ID. LOANVAL.
       AUTHOR. ENTERPRISE-BANKING-SYSTEMS.
      *****************************************************************
      * LOAN VALIDATION SUBPROGRAM                                   *
      * VALIDATES PAYMENT INPUT RECORDS                              *
      *****************************************************************
       DATA DIVISION.
       WORKING-STORAGE SECTION.
       01  WS-VALIDATION-CHECKS.
           05  WS-LOAN-NUM-VALID       PIC X VALUE 'Y'.
           05  WS-AMOUNT-VALID         PIC X VALUE 'Y'.
           05  WS-DATE-VALID           PIC X VALUE 'Y'.

       LINKAGE SECTION.
       01  LS-PAYMENT-REC.
           COPY PMTINPUT.
       01  LS-VALIDATION-STATUS        PIC X.
       01  LS-ERROR-MESSAGE            PIC X(100).
       01  LS-RETURN-CODE              PIC S9(4) COMP.

       PROCEDURE DIVISION USING LS-PAYMENT-REC
                                LS-VALIDATION-STATUS
                                LS-ERROR-MESSAGE
                                LS-RETURN-CODE.
       0000-MAIN-VALIDATION.
           MOVE 'Y' TO LS-VALIDATION-STATUS
           MOVE SPACES TO LS-ERROR-MESSAGE
           MOVE ZERO TO LS-RETURN-CODE

           PERFORM 1000-VALIDATE-LOAN-NUMBER
           PERFORM 2000-VALIDATE-AMOUNT
           PERFORM 3000-VALIDATE-DATE

           IF WS-LOAN-NUM-VALID = 'N' OR
              WS-AMOUNT-VALID = 'N' OR
              WS-DATE-VALID = 'N'
               MOVE 'N' TO LS-VALIDATION-STATUS
               MOVE 8 TO LS-RETURN-CODE
           END-IF

           GOBACK.

       1000-VALIDATE-LOAN-NUMBER.
           IF PI-LOAN-NUMBER = SPACES OR LOW-VALUES
               MOVE 'N' TO WS-LOAN-NUM-VALID
               MOVE 'INVALID LOAN NUMBER' TO LS-ERROR-MESSAGE
           END-IF.

       2000-VALIDATE-AMOUNT.
           IF PI-PAYMENT-AMOUNT NOT > ZERO
               MOVE 'N' TO WS-AMOUNT-VALID
               MOVE 'INVALID PAYMENT AMOUNT' TO LS-ERROR-MESSAGE
           END-IF.

       3000-VALIDATE-DATE.
           IF PI-PAYMENT-DATE NOT NUMERIC
               MOVE 'N' TO WS-DATE-VALID
               MOVE 'INVALID PAYMENT DATE' TO LS-ERROR-MESSAGE
           END-IF.
