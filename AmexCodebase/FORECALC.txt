       IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
       PROGRAM-ID. FORECALC.
      *****************************************************************
      * FORECLOSURE TIMELINE CALCULATION UTILITY                     *
      *****************************************************************
       DATA DIVISION.
       WORKING-STORAGE SECTION.
       01  WS-CURRENT-DATE             PIC 9(8).

       LINKAGE SECTION.
       01  LS-LOAN-NUMBER              PIC X(20).
       01  LS-NOTICE-DATE              PIC 9(8).
       01  LS-SALE-DATE                PIC 9(8).
       01  LS-DAYS-TO-SALE             PIC 9(5) COMP.
       01  LS-CALCULATION-STATUS       PIC X.
       01  LS-RETURN-CODE              PIC S9(4) COMP.

       PROCEDURE DIVISION USING LS-LOAN-NUMBER
                                LS-NOTICE-DATE
                                LS-SALE-DATE
                                LS-DAYS-TO-SALE
                                LS-CALCULATION-STATUS
                                LS-RETURN-CODE.
       0000-MAIN.
           MOVE 'Y' TO LS-CALCULATION-STATUS
           MOVE ZERO TO LS-RETURN-CODE
           ACCEPT WS-CURRENT-DATE FROM DATE YYYYMMDD
           MOVE WS-CURRENT-DATE TO LS-NOTICE-DATE
           MOVE 120 TO LS-DAYS-TO-SALE
           CALL 'DATEADD' USING WS-CURRENT-DATE
                                LS-DAYS-TO-SALE
                                LS-SALE-DATE
                                LS-CALCULATION-STATUS
           GOBACK.
